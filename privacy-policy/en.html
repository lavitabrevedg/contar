Contar Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 · Last updated: September 9, 2026

PeachTreeStudio (“we”) operates the Contar mobile game (“the App”). This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. Purpose of Processing

We process information only as needed to identify anonymous users and save or restore game progress, provide rewarded ads and prevent fraud, and respond to privacy inquiries, rights requests, and data deletion requests.

We do not directly collect your name, telephone number, postal address, contact list, photos, camera or microphone data, or precise location for the App’s core features.

3. Advertising and Consent Choices

The App may provide rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads SDK. Where consent is required by law, Google’s consent-management process provides choices related to personalized advertising. You may also change advertising-ID related settings on your device.

4. Retention

We retain information while needed for the purposes above or until we process a deletion request, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Anonymous identifiers and game progress are retained while the service is provided or until deletion is processed. Support records may be retained for the period needed to resolve the inquiry or a dispute. On-device settings remain on your device until you delete App data or uninstall the App.

5. Deletion

We delete information without undue delay when the retention period ends or the processing purpose is achieved. Electronic data is deleted so that it cannot be restored, or is processed under the deletion procedures of the applicable service provider.

7. International Processing

Because we use Firebase and Google Mobile Ads, information may be processed on servers in countries where Google or its service providers operate, including the United States. The categories and purposes are described in Section 6.

8. Security

We limit access to systems that process personal information and use the security features of Firebase and Google services as appropriate.

9. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. For anonymous game data, we may request minimal information needed to identify the data, such as your App version, device information, or approximate time of use.

To request deletion of game progress or related data, email us with the subject “Contar data deletion request.”
